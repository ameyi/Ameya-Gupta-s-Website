ABOUT ME

High school senior passionate about building, learning, and teaching.

I'm a senior at Jesuit High School in Folsom, California with a strong passion for electrical engineering, artificial intelligence, competitive mathematics, and robotics. I love building things that solve real problems and teaching others what I’ve learned along the way.

I founded AI Explorers, an initiative that introduces younger students to artificial intelligence through interactive lessons. I also run an educational Instagram account (@ameyagogogo) with over 1,000 followers where I teach SAT math strategies.

Outside of academics, I compete in VEX Robotics as a programmer and content creator for team Cognitaur 29712C (qualified for the World Championship). I’ve also completed a competitive NASA internship in aerospace research. I enjoy combining technical skills with creativity — whether that’s building web projects with Three.js, editing robotics videos, or designing curriculum.

In my free time, I like playing chess, working on personal coding projects, and spending time outdoors. I also run two YouTube channels: ProCoderGaming and COGNITAUR.
